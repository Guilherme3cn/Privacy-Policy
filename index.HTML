Privacy Policy - JumpFox

Developer: G&B Games
Last updated: December 2025

This Privacy Policy explains how the game JumpFox ("the App") handles user data. JumpFox is developed and published by G&B Games.

JumpFox is a single-player offline game that does not collect, store, process, or share any personal or sensitive user data.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

The App does not collect any type of information, including:

  • Personal information (name, email, phone number, address, etc.)
  • Device or system information
  • Location data (GPS, IP-based, coarse or fine location)
  • Gameplay analytics or usage statistics
  • Financial or payment information
  • Advertising identifiers
  • Cookies or tracking technologies

JumpFox does not require login, registration, or account creation.

2. Local Game Data Only

JumpFox stores only basic gameplay progress on the user's device, such as:

  • Current level
  • Score
  • Local game settings

This data remains on the device and is not transmitted to any server or third party.

3. No Internet Connection Required

The App can be played entirely offline. JumpFox does not:

  • Connect to external servers
  • Send or receive data over the internet
  • Use APIs, analytics systems, or external services

4. No Ads or In-App Purchases

JumpFox is a paid game. It contains:

  • No advertisements
  • No in-app purchases
  • No third-party SDKs

5. Sharing of Information

Since we do not collect any data, no information is shared with third parties.

6. Children's Privacy

JumpFox contains no inappropriate content and is suitable for players of all ages.

As no personal data is collected, the App complies with applicable privacy regulations such as COPPA and GDPR principles, as well as Google Play's data policies, even though it is not part of the Designed for Families program.

7. Policy Updates

We may update this Privacy Policy if needed. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated date at the top.
