Privacy Policy - Trail of Light

Developer: G&B Games
Last updated: December 2025

This Privacy Policy explains how the game Trail of Light ("the App") handles user data. Trail of Light is developed and published by G&B Games.

The App is designed to minimize data collection and to comply with Google Play's User Data, Families, and Ads policies.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

The App does not collect any of the following:

  • Personal identifiers such as name, email, phone number, or address
  • Precise location data (GPS)
  • Contacts, photos, or other device-stored content
  • Payment information
  • Advertising identifiers for profiling

Trail of Light does not require login, registration, or account creation.

2. Game Data Stored Locally

The App stores only basic gameplay progress on the user's device, such as:

  • Level progression
  • Scores
  • Local game settings

This data stays on the device and is not transmitted to our servers or third parties.

3. Optional Diagnostics

If crash reporting or basic analytics are enabled by the platform (e.g., Google Play services), the App may receive device-level diagnostics to improve stability and performance. These may include:

  • Device model and OS version
  • Country/region (non-precise)
  • App version and crash logs
  • Aggregate gameplay events (e.g., level progress) without personal identifiers

No sensitive personal data is collected. You can disable analytics/crash collection in your device settings where supported.

4. Ads and Purchases

The App contains no in-app purchases. If ads are displayed, ad partners may receive device-level identifiers and usage signals solely to deliver and measure ads, in accordance with their privacy policies. You can manage ad personalization in your device settings.

5. Sharing of Information

We do not sell personal information. If diagnostics or ads are enabled, data may be shared only with trusted service providers (such as crash/analytics tools or ad networks) acting on our instructions to support the App. They must protect the data and may not use it for their own purposes.

6. Children's Privacy

The App is suitable for general audiences. It does not collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

7. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect data. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure.

8. International Transfers

If platform diagnostics or ads are enabled, data may be processed in countries outside your own. We apply safeguards appropriate to the destination's requirements.

9. Your Choices

  • Manage ad personalization via your device settings (e.g., Google settings > Ads/Privacy).
  • Disable analytics/crash reporting where your device supports it.
  • Request access or deletion of diagnostic data by contacting us.

10. Updates to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy. Any changes will be posted on this page with the updated date shown above.
